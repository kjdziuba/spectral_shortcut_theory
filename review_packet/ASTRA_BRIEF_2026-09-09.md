## 1. The paper in one paragraph, and the candidate thesis

The paper studies compositional pipelines F = g_φ ∘ f_θ: a low-capacity per-pixel spectral module f_θ (linear S→K; on breast QCL S = 942, K = 64, C_f = 61,108 parameters) feeding a high-capacity spatial module g_φ (a ViT, "BlockViT-v2"; C_g = 18,271,540 at width M = 192, ratio 299). Two gradient-flow results: (Thm 1) the top-curvature ratio D_curv = λ_max(G_φφ)/λ_max(G_θθ) grows at least linearly in M at Kaiming init, because λ_max(G_θθ) ≤ L̃²λ_max(Σ_X) (Lemma 18) while λ_max(G_φφ) has an Ω(M) floor via a rank-one witness (Lemma 16); (Thm 2) if the residual decays as C/(1+μt), spectral displacement by the fitting time is ≤ (B_T C/μ)·log(C/δ) — "finite-time spectral starvation". It proposes a diagnostic EGR = ‖∇_θL‖/‖∇_φL‖ and tests the picture on synthetic data (Exp 1.1–1.3) and real IR tissue images (breast, prostate).

Pre-inversion framing (compiled §8.4, 2026-09-01): on breast fold 0 under AdamW, "every frozen arm beats every joint arm at both widths" on final-5-epoch val macro-F1 (joint − frozen-random = −0.110 at M=48, −0.085 at M=192); the joint spectral module moves 55–60% from init "and this movement hurts"; under plain SGD joint ≈ frozen-random (Theorem 2's *functional* prediction, read informally — Thm 27 is a continuous a.e. gradient-flow result and discrete SGD is listed OPEN in §2, so the SGD arm is a control *motivated by* the theorem, never an instance of it); the AdamW failure is "harmful drift, not lazy pinning".

Post-inversion framing (round 3, 2026-09-02, verified): on best-of-60-epochs val F1 the sign flips — joint-linear 0.873/0.858 vs frozen-random 0.840/0.817 (+0.033/+0.041); joint-MLP has the highest best-val of any arm (0.900/0.897, sd 0.0004 — see the ceiling caveat in §3d, and note that an untabulated frozen-PCA run peaks higher still at 0.9322). What survives: joint arms are unstable late in training (val-F1 sd over the last 20 epochs 0.145–0.219 vs 0.006–0.023 for frozen-PCA; one joint run ends at 0.242 from a peak of 0.896), any time-averaged window still favours frozen, and the SGD joint−frozen-random gap is within the pre-declared ±0.02 margin at BOTH widths on best-val (+0.002 at M=48, +0.011 at M=192) — i.e. width-robust on best-val. It is NOT metric-robust: on the final-5-epoch metric the same gaps are +0.028 (M=48) and −0.057 (M=192), the latter ~3× outside the margin.

Candidate thesis (what we want sharpened; we are not attached to it):

> In spectral→spatial pipelines the low-capacity spectral module is not "pinned" by joint training; it is mis-directed. At initialization on real spectra its curvature concentrates in a handful of directions (top-40 of 61,108 carry 0.83 ± 0.12 of the θ-block trace; per seed 0.69–1.00, n=3 — one seed is at essentially 100%, another at ~70%, so this is a tendency, not a constant) along the mean-spectrum direction of a rank-one input Gram (effective rank 1.07/942), so class-contrast directions receive 11.7–28.0× less curvature (median 20.5) — starvation is direction-wise, not norm-wise. That contrast measurement rests on n=6 numbers from 2 of 3 seeds, 3 of 4 batches, ONE class pair (CancerEpi−CAS), ONE width (M=192), at initialization only. Under plain SGD, joint training is descriptively equivalent to leaving the module at random init: the joint−frozen-random best-val gap is within the pre-declared ±0.02 margin at both widths (+0.002, +0.011). No TOST has been run (n=3, sd up to 0.075), the final-5 metric does not reproduce it at M=192 (−0.057), and this is an SGD *control*, not an application of Theorem 2 (discrete SGD is OPEN, §2). Under AdamW the module drifts 55–95% and produces peak-then-collapse trajectories, so freezing (random or PCA) trades peak accuracy for stability. The scalar EGR is blind to all of this (it rises 0.72→4.3 while the joint arm degrades).

The honest problem: the practical message the paper was built on ("freeze the spectral module; joint training starves it") is no longer supported by the strongest real-data experiment on its peak metric, and the theory (unregularized, contained gradient flow) does not describe the AdamW regime where the interesting instability lives.

## 2. Frozen theory (v1.1): proved vs assumed vs open

Compiled labels in parentheses; full statements are in the attached packet. Model: per-pixel CE averaged over N = N_data·H·W; residual r = N^{-1/2}(softmax(ŷ_n) − y_n); J_• = N^{-1/2}∂ŷ/∂• so ∇_•L = J_•ᵀr; GGN G = JᵀH_τJ, 0 ⪯ H_τ ⪯ I.

Assumptions. Ass 1 (linear f_θ; nonlinear f_θ "verified empirically" only). Ass 3 (regularity: g_φ twice continuously differentiable jointly in (φ, Z) with L-Lipschitz gradients, relaxed a.e. for piecewise-smooth models). Its *second-derivative* content is unused — the source says only "no quantitative bound in this paper consumes second derivatives" (`03_setup.tex` L51-60) — but the assumption itself IS consumed: Cor 28's statement invokes "the smooth branch of Assumption 3" so that t ↦ L(t) is differentiable along the joint flow, which is what licenses the energy identity behind the bound (`05_theorem2_twoscale.tex` L221-235). Do not repeat the earlier claim that no proof consumes Ass 3. Ass 4 (uncentered Σ_X := N^{-1}Σx_nxₙᵀ with λ_max ≤ Λ_X; restated as an empirical bound after a round-1 dependent-pixel counterexample). Ass 6 (C_g/C_f → ∞; the source states outright "Assumption~\ref{ass:gap} is not used in any proof" — it delimits the regime in which the theorems are informative rather than merely true). Ass 7 (width-uniform L̃ ≥ sup ‖∂g_φ/∂Z‖_op over the training regime; the paper says RMT does not deliver it — ‖W_1‖_op = Θ(√(M/K)) diverges — it is a cross-layer cancellation ASSUMED in general and PROVED only in expectation at init for a one-hidden-layer ReLU head; compactness of Z_reg for attention is imposed). Ass 24 (‖r(t)‖ ≤ C/(1+μt); C, μ not uniform over any family; μ growth with C_g conjectured and tested via μ̂). Trajectory containment (unnumbered): φ(t) ∈ Φ_reg, Z(t) ∈ Z_reg for t ≤ T, which makes B_T ≤ L̃√λ_max(Σ_X) M-independent; Rem 23 concedes it "cannot be assumed" in the unregularized separable regime and that weight decay on θ alters the flow (displacement θ_0(1−e^{−2αT}) even at r ≡ 0) — so Thm 2 applies only with θ in a zero-weight-decay group.

Statements.
- Lemma 18 (cap), PROVED, deterministic: λ_max(G_θθ) ≤ ‖J_θ‖²_op ≤ L̃²λ_max(Σ_X) =: C_θ, using ‖∂Z/∂θ‖²_op = λ_max(I_K ⊗ Σ_X) = λ_max(Σ_X) exactly.
- Lemma 16 (floor), PROVED given the head hypothesis (softmax-weighted penultimate Gram S_h^p has λ_max ≥ q_hM_h w.p. ≥ 1−δ_h): λ_max(G_φφ) ≥ λ_max(S_h^p) deterministically via a rank-one witness, so E[λ_max(G_φφ)] ≥ (1−δ_h)q_hM_h. Per-vector norms alone give Θ(1); the degenerate case (odd activation, σ_b = 0) is not attempted.
- Theorem 1 (Thm 14): under Ass 1, 4, 7 + head hypothesis, D_curv ≥ c_1M − c_2 in expectation over Kaiming init (c_2 = 0 suffices); for C_g = Θ(M²), ≥ c_1'√(C_g/C_f) − c_2. Head hypothesis is PROVED only for the one-hidden-layer ReLU head with σ_b > 0 (Prop 38); for CNN/ViT/deeper heads it is "an explicit assumption … checked empirically".
- Prop 38 (verified instance), PROVED at init: (i) λ_max(S_h^p) ≥ q_hM_h w.p. ≥ 1/2, q_h = c_pσ_b²/(8π); (ii) E[λ_max(G_θθ)] ≤ N_cls σ_w⁴ tr(Σ_X) =: C_G; (iii) w.p. ≥ 3/8, D_curv ≥ (q_h/(8C_G))M_h and E[D_curv] ≥ (3/64)(q_h/C_G)M_h. Rem 40 concedes the spike comes from the mean feature direction and holds even if every z_n = 0: D_curv "measures readiness to move, not the value of what it computes".
- Theorem 2 (Thm 27), PROVED given Ass 24 + containment, for a.e. gradient flow: ‖θ(T) − θ_0‖ ≤ (B_TC/μ)log(1+μT); at T_δ ≤ (C/δ−1)/μ, displacement ≤ (B_TC/μ)log(C/δ) = O(ε log(1/δ)), ε := B_T/μ. NOT claimed: t → ∞; discrete SGD (open); Adam (scoped out: "joint-vs-frozen gaps observed under Adam are evidence about the phenomenon, not applications of the theorem"). Thm 1 and Thm 2 are logically independent; the disparity → μ-growth bridge is "the most valuable missing theorem".
- Cor 28 (attribution), PROVED: A_θ(T) := ∫‖∇_θL‖²/(L(0)−L(T)) ≤ B_T²C²/(μ(L(0)−L(T))); the paper says the a-priori version "may exceed 1 and thus be vacuous at realistic constants".
- Prop 31 (class-prior NTK), PROVED, instantaneous at init: wᵀΘ̂w ≥ ‖h̄‖² ≥ q̄M_h along the class-prior contrast; does not control the trajectory.
- Prop 33, PROVED: ‖∇_θL(t)‖ ≤ B_TC/(1+μt) (EGR numerator only; the ratio "is an empirical object"; EGR(0) ~ √(C_f/C_g) and the τ ∈ [0.05, 0.1] alert are heuristics).

Ledger. PROVED: Lem 18; witness bound; Lem 16 | head hypothesis; Thm 14 | head hypothesis + Ass 7; Prop 38; Thm 27 | Ass 24 + containment; Cor 28; Prop 31; Prop 33. ## 7. Known weaknesses (candid)

1. The best-val inversion. The strongest real-data experiment no longer shows "freezing helps" on peak performance; joint peaks higher then collapses. 2. Theorem 2 is vacuous on real data by 1e4–2e5×, and containment is violated by 50–10,000× Jacobian growth. Its real-data role is structural, not numerical.
3. ## 9. Appendix

### 9.1 Glossary

- θ, φ: parameters of f_θ (linear S→K, vec(W)) and g_φ (spatial ViT/CNN/MLP); C_f = dim θ, C_g = dim φ. M: width of g_φ. K: reduced dimension (64 in E3c/geometry; 128 in companion/R1). S: input dimension of f_θ = channels × wavenumbers. **S = 942 for breast QCL AND for prostate QCL** (3 channels × 314 wavenumbers); do not confuse S with the wavenumber count. The figures 314 (prostate QCL) and 326 (FTIR) quoted elsewhere in this brief and in the logs are WAVENUMBER counts, and `PROGRESS.md` L1584 records them correctly as such ("FTIR S=326 wn (QCL 314)"). The prostate geometry run confirms it directly: it reports the same C_f = 61,108 as breast and a 942-dimensional input Gram ("eff_rank=1.02 of 942", `results/exp1_8_prostate.log` L7-10).
- Σ_X: uncentered N^{-1}Σx_nxₙᵀ; eff-rank = trace/λ_max. G = JᵀH_τJ: GGN; G_θθ, G_φφ its blocks. D_curv := λ_max(G_φφ)/λ_max(G_θθ) (+∞ if denominator 0).
- L̃: width-uniform bound on ‖∂g_φ/∂Z‖_op (Ass 7); C_θ := L̃²λ_max(Σ_X). B_T := sup_{t≤T}‖J_θ(t)‖_op ≤ √C_θ under containment; B_p99 = empirical 99th-percentile realized gain used in the E3c bound.
- r(t): RMS per-pixel residual; envelope ‖r(t)‖ ≤ C/(1+μt) (Ass 24); μ = shortcut fitting rate; Ĉ = max of the first 10 steps; μ̂ = sup{μ : r(t) ≤ Ĉ/(1+μt)} on a rolling-median(25) residual.
- δ: residual level defining T_δ := inf{t : ‖r(t)‖ ≤ δ} ≤ (C/δ−1)/μ. ε := B_T/μ (Thm 2 displacement O(ε log(1/δ))).
- EGR(t) := ‖∇_θL‖/‖∇_φL‖; alert when < τ·EGR(0), τ ∈ [0.05, 0.1]. thm2_bound := (B_p99·Ĉ/μ̂)·log(Ĉ/r_T). diss_share_theta := Σ‖∇_θL‖²/(Σ‖∇_θL‖² + Σ‖∇_φL‖²). S_h^p := N^{-1}Σ p_min^{(n)}h_nh_nᵀ; h̄ = mean penultimate feature.
- Compiled labels: Ass 1 linear; 3 regularity; 4 second moment; 6 capacity gap; 7 input Jacobian; 24 residual decay; Def 10 EGR; Def 12 D_curv; Thm 14 = "Thm 1"; Lem 16 floor; Lem 18 cap; Def 26 fitting time; Thm 27 = "Thm 2"; Cor 28; Prop 31; Prop 33; Def 36 alert; Prop 38 verified instance.
