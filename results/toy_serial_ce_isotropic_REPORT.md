## 8. Immediate-stop convention (`a0 >= m`)

54 cases with `a0 >= m` use the matched-threshold rule and stop at `T_m = 0`: `a(T_m) = a0`, displacement `0`, suppression `0`, reversal margin `2a0 - m > 0` and reversal error `0` (agreeing with the limit, since `a0 >= m > m/2`).
