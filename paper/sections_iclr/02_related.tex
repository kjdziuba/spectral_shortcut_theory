\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Modality competition and laziness.}
Joint training of multi-branch models is known to under-train the branch
that is slower to fit: \citet{wang2020what} and \citet{peng2022balanced}
document the effect and propose gradient blending and modulation,
\citet{du2023unimodal} name the under-used branch lazy, and
\citet{huang2022modality} prove competition for explicit late-fusion
networks. Our setting is a single input passed serially through two modules;
the asymmetry is positional rather than between parallel branches, and our
bounds concern module displacement rather than feature selection.

\paragraph{Shortcut and starvation theory.}
\citet{pezeshki2021gradient} prove, in a linearized regime with coupled
feature dynamics, that a fast feature starves a slow one of gradient. We
bound displacement of a whole module in a compositional pipeline and take
the residual envelope as a hypothesis; neither result subsumes the other. The
residual-excitation statement of Section~\ref{sec:dynamics} makes precise
why curvature anisotropy alone does not imply starvation.

\paragraph{Curvature, width and normalization.}
The width-linear growth of the top Gauss--Newton eigenvalue in
proportional-width networks is the mean-field law of
\citet{karakida2019universal}; block heterogeneity of the Hessian is the
optimizer-side antecedent of \citet{zhang2024adam}, and the top-eigenvector
structure $u\otimes\E[x]$ appears in \citet{papyan2020prevalence}. The
scale invariance of layers feeding batch normalization, and its effect on
effective learning rates, is established in \citet{vanlaarhoven2017l2}; we
use it as a diagnosis of one model, not as a new mechanism.

\paragraph{Lazy training and implicit bias in serial models.}
Our nonlinear existence result is an explicit-constant instance of lazy
training in the standard parameterization
\citep{chizat2019lazy,astra_sohldickstein2020,yang2021tensor}, proved with
the gate-flip argument of \citet{astra_du2019} and the kernel-tracking
estimates of \citet{astra_lee2019,astra_arora2019}. The solvable serial
instance rests on the layer-balance invariant of \citet{astra_du2018balance}
and sits beside the exact dynamics of \citet{astra_saxe2014} and the
architecture- and initialization-dependent implicit biases of
\citet{astra_yun2021,astra_moroshko2020,astra_berthier2023}; it adds a
matched-margin encoder-update bound, a frozen comparator and a reversal test
in one system, not a new mechanism of implicit bias.

\paragraph{Frozen features in practice.}
Last-layer retraining on frozen features recovers robustness to spurious
correlations \citep{kirichenko2023dfr}; some layers barely move during
training \citep{zhang2022layers}; and \citet{coil2025freezing} hypothesize
that freezing acts as regularization. Our matched-protocol study is a
caution for this literature: the apparent benefit of freezing in our
production model was a property of an unmatched protocol.
