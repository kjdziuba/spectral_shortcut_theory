\section{Limitations and conclusion}
\label{sec:limitations}

We have separated three ingredients of selective encoder adaptation in
serial models and given conditional bounds for each: curvature disparity
under a head hypothesis, displacement under a residual envelope, and a
finite-horizon attribution that needs coercivity only on the residual
actually visited. The solvable instance shows the bounds biting; the
production model shows the diagnostics failing in instructive ways; the
matched protocol shows an apparent training effect dissolving.

The limits are these. The theorems assume a linear encoder, and the
strongest constructive guarantees hold for deliberately scoped models: an
affine system, a shallow ReLU head with a numerically vacuous width
threshold, and a scalar serial instance. The production study does not
establish their mechanism; it establishes that the scalar disparity is the
wrong lens on rank-one inputs, that a width trend can be a normalization
gain, and that the head hypothesis is unmet, with three-width slopes that are
descriptive rather than asymptotic. The residual experiment is a probe-batch
diagnostic under Adam: eleven checkpoints cannot certify an almost-everywhere
condition, and its comparison against an isotropic residual awaits a
reference computed in the class-contrast subspace of dimension $N(C-1)$.
The matched study is one width, three seeds, one validation fold, no test
set and two matched pairs; it rules out reading the earlier gap as
architectural, not the existence of an effect. Mitigation strategies,
including per-module learning rates, gradient modulation and freezing, are
not evaluated as prescriptions here, and the matched study is a reason to
distrust untuned comparisons of them. What the paper adds beyond
lazy-training, optimization-metric and feature-competition results is the
distinction itself, with measurements showing where each ingredient fails
to transfer. The open problem is a same-sequence phase certificate: a
training run on which the phase membership, its energy fraction $w_E$ and
the bound of Proposition~\ref{prop:astra_phase} are evaluated on one
sequence, or a full-batch flow instance where the energy identity is exact.
