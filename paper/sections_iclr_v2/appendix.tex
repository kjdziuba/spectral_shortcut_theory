\section{Proofs of Theorem~\ref{thm:serial}}
\label{app:proofs}

Theorem~\ref{thm:serial} combines three statements proved below in their
original form: the serial instance at the special initialization
(Theorem~\ref{thm:astra_serial}), its general and isotropic initialization
(Corollary~\ref{cor:astra_isotropic}), and the readout normalization
(Proposition~\ref{prop:astra_serial_control}). Part (a) of
Theorem~\ref{thm:serial} is \eqref{eq:astra_iso_update} together with the
finite-horizon gap \eqref{eq:astra_iso_gap}; part (b) is the reversal
statement of Corollary~\ref{cor:astra_isotropic} including
\eqref{eq:astra_iso_error}; part (c) is
Proposition~\ref{prop:astra_serial_control} with $\alpha_M=M^{-1/2}$, whose
proof applies to any zero-sum initialization of the normalized readouts
because only the aggregate $b$ enters the flow. The rate-$\kappa$ variant
quoted in Section~\ref{sec:theorem} follows by the same differentiation with
$\dot\beta_j=\kappa v\varrho$. The special-initialization remark is
\eqref{eq:astra_serial_time} and the GGN display of
Theorem~\ref{thm:astra_serial}. Notation: $\varrho=(1+e^{q})^{-1}$ is the
logistic residual, $\Psi(q)=q+e^q-1$, and $G_{\thetap\thetap}$,
$G_{\phip\phip}$ are the Gauss--Newton blocks of the scalar-logit loss in
the parameter blocks $\thetap=(a,v)$ and $\phip=\beta$.

\section{Exact-model verification}
\label{app:verification}

The closed forms of Appendix~\ref{app:proofs} were checked against a full
$(2+M)$-parameter integration of the gradient flow. The tables below are
reproduced from the verification record of the earlier draft
(\texttt{code/experiments/toy\_serial\_ce.py},
\texttt{toy\_serial\_ce\_isotropic.py}); the maximum relative discrepancy over
the four endpoint quantities in the $525$ non-trivial isotropic cases is
$1.26\times10^{-11}$, the maximum trajectory-identity discrepancy is
$1.5\times10^{-10}$, and no bound is violated in any case. The quantities and
error conventions are those stated in each caption.

\section{Scope limitations from a production model}
\label{app:scope}

This appendix reproduces, from the earlier diagnostic draft of this work,
the measurements on a production spectral--spatial segmentation model that
are summarized in Section~\ref{sec:limitations}. They concern initialization
curvature and a controlled retraining study; they do not measure the
mechanism of Theorem~\ref{thm:serial} and are included to document why
scalar curvature is not the diagnostic used in this paper and why the
earlier apparent freezing advantage is not evidence for or against it. The
data are breast-tissue QCL micro-array cores with a prostate QCL replication.
Theorem and proposition numbers quoted inside this appendix refer to the
earlier draft's framework and are retained only where the sentence is a
scope statement about that framework.
