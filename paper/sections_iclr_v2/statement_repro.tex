Code, configurations and audit scripts, with seeds and exact commands, are
provided in the anonymized repository accompanying this submission. Every
number in the paper traces to a named results file;

including the two that do not exit cleanly. Complete hypotheses and proofs
are in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs}, with the numerical verification in
Appendix~\ref{app:verification}. Generators, calibration, architectures,
data, patient splits and training protocols are described in
Appendices~\ref{app:exp2}, \ref{app:exp3} and~\ref{app:scope}.
