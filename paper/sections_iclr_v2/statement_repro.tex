Code, configurations and audit scripts, with seeds and exact commands, are
provided in the anonymized repository accompanying this submission. Every
number in the paper traces to a named results file;
Appendix~\ref{app:audits} lists the verification scripts and their outcomes,
including the two that do not exit cleanly. Complete hypotheses and proofs
are in Appendices~\ref{app:proofs} and~\ref{app:math}, the diagnostic of
Appendix~\ref{app:egr} included. Generators, architectures, data, splits and
training protocols are described in Appendices~\ref{app:synthetic}
and~\ref{app:production}.
